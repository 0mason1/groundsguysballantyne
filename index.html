Who We Are
A Corporate Career Traded for a Greener Calling

Kevin Murphy spent 25 years learning what world-class customer service looks like from the inside of corporate America. During the COVID-19 lockdown, he found himself pouring more time into his true passion — landscaping his own yard and designing outdoor spaces.

That clarity led him to start The Grounds Guys of Ballantyne — combining the service standards of corporate America with the hands-on care of a local small business. Kevin is a hands-on owner who regularly visits properties himself, personally checks in on his team's work, and has been known to show up on a day off to finish a job when equipment fails.

As a Neighborly company, every job is backed by the Done Right Promise — if it's not right, we make it right, full stop.
